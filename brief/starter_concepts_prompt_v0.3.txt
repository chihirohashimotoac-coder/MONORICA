Create a BRAND NEW original creature-RPG starter evolution concept sheet after a full-roster visual design study. Reference images 1 and 2 are official Pokémon starter evolution boards, provided ONLY to study high-level character-design grammar: purposeful silhouettes, varied eye shapes and personalities, clean readable color blocking, anatomical motifs that explain an action, and real transformation through evolution. Do not reproduce their species, exact silhouettes, signature faces, clothing or motifs. Do not redraw any reference creature. The output must be NINE NEW original creature designs. The earlier project concepts were generic cute animals with elemental accessories and final forms that merely enlarged babies; move decisively beyond that. Aim for the design sophistication and charm of modern Pokémon, with cute distinctive first stages and compelling strong final stages. Artwork: confident clean colored ink contours, restrained flat cel shading, no gradients, no painted fur, no glossy toy rendering, no decorative grass platforms, no background scenery. Warm plain white sheet. SQUARE canvas, orderly THREE COLUMNS and THREE ROWS, with spacious separation; column A is grass, B is fire, C is water. Top row = first stage, middle = middle stage, bottom = final evolution. Only small simple A, B, C headings above columns. All nine figures fully visible, no overlaps or cropping. Give final stages more space and varied strong proportions. Do not give all creatures identical smiling faces, huge sparkling eyes, mammalian ears or a common four-legged body plan. Use clear readable eyes appropriate to personality. Each family has one strong original integrated anatomical idea with continuity across all three stages.
COLUMN A, GRASS SEED-POD ARMADILLO: concept is a seed keeper that curls into a seed and later protects seedlings and allies. A1 is a very compact rounded low creature, with a short warm-clay face partially peeking from its own smooth moss-green SEED POD back shell, a single visible curved shell seam, tiny forepaws, small ear nubs, and quiet curious slightly sleepy black oval eyes. It should feel like a charming little living seed, not a hedgehog wearing a pile of leaves. A2 grows sturdy digging forearms and a longer torso; the shell seam opens into TWO large clean pod panels that shelter its shoulders. A3 is a broad low guardian quadruped with strong rounded spade-like forefeet and a large seed-pod back shell unfolded into THREE elegant protective curved leaf panels. Clear powerful adult silhouette, a shorter mature face with calm confident eyes, substantial body mass distinct from the small first stage. Shell panels are its anatomy, not armor or clothing. Dominant moss green, warm clay, cream; minimal pale seed accents. No tree on the back, no flower decorations, no woodland textures.
COLUMN B, FIRE BELLOWS GROUNDBIRD: concept is a bird whose wing strokes fan the ember inside its breast, growing into a living forge. B1 is a small charming two-legged groundbird with a soft triangular charcoal-gray body, a short amber beak, cream cheek patches, little paddle-shaped wings, coral feet and ONE simple amber diamond chest marking suggesting a warm inner ember. A tiny bent smoke-curl feather is part of the head silhouette. Its expression is concentrated and a little comically determined, not the same smile as A or C. Keep it mostly charcoal and cream, not an orange chicken. B2 has longer legs, a more upright neck, wider flat wings and a brighter clear chest shape; it visibly learns to fan and focus heat. B3 is a tall powerful BIPEDAL groundbird with a bold lantern-shaped breast, very broad folded bellows-like WINGS that are feathered anatomy, robust bird legs and a poised wings-partly-open stance. The chest glows as one restrained flat amber area, and a few simple flame-shaped feather tips accent the wings. Its whole posture and wing shape communicate fanning and controlled powerful heat. No tools, hammer, human clothing, literal furnace doors, boxing gloves, crown, staff, fire lion mane or flame-covered humanoid. Adult face confident and alert, still charming. Dominant charcoal, warm ivory, amber with tiny coral accents.
COLUMN C, WATER BOAT-TAIL NEWT: concept is a river guide that floats and steers with its own boat-shaped tail fin. C1 is a cute low elongated AQUATIC NEWT, with a broad flat wedge-shaped head, widely spaced horizontal dark eyes, a tiny curious mouth, four short webbed limbs, pale blue body and ONE soft cream jaw area. Its broad fleshy tail fin curves upward into a shallow V-rimmed boat shape behind its own body; it can curl that fin underneath itself to float. Tail is its own organ, never a separate wooden boat or accessory. No external gill horns, yellow forehead crest, round tapir ears or long snout. C2 grows longer, with a wider paddle tail and a poised leaning-forward swimming stance; a clean navy stripe connects shoulder to tail. C3 is a sleek long low QUADRUPED river-guide newt with robust webbed forefeet, a confidently lifted broad head, a VERY LARGE graceful curved tail fin that rises to one side like a steering sail and forms a distinctive sweeping adult silhouette. Keep head and expression friendly and mature; make the tail structure the main focus rather than extra jewelry or head crests. Its own skin forms the fin. Dominant pale cyan, dark river teal and cream, with at most one tiny coral accent. No literal life jacket, detachable raft, frog ninja scarf or human uniform.
Make the three columns unmistakably different in silhouette: compact seed guardian, tall bellows bird, elongated boat-tail river guide. Evolution must change posture, proportion and function, while retaining a recognizable core anatomical motif and palette. Each stage must feel designed, memorable, plausible as a game partner, and readable as a small sprite. This is an ORIGINAL design concept board, not production-ready sprites.
