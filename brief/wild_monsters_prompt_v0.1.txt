Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original creature-design comparison board for a single-player monster-collecting RPG. This is concept art for review, not a production sprite sheet.
Create exactly SIX distinct, original, charming early-game wild creature characters on one landscape board, three columns by two rows. Each character has its own spacious white cell, full body fully visible, no cropping or overlaps. A small elegant dark letter above each character: top row A, B, C; bottom row D, E, F. Those six letters are the ONLY text. No borders or scenery, very subtle grounding shadows.
Visual direction: polished contemporary Japanese family-friendly creature-RPG concept illustration, clean controlled dark colored outlines, appealing solid silhouettes, flat local colors with crisp two-tone cel shading, expressive faces, simplified stylized anatomy. Clear at small size. These are approachable small early-stage wildlife companions, not giant legendary creatures. Draw six unique faces and proportions, not the same round plush mascot repeated six times. Design creature bodies, not animals wearing human costumes. All characters original, with no existing franchise character, logo, or recognizable signature design.

A (top left) — seed squirrel, neutral element. A lively amber-and-cream squirrel with an upright alert pose, small triangular ears, lively hazel eyes and a tiny smile. Its oversized upright curled tail has a clear three-scalloped seedpod-like outer silhouette, chestnut darker underside, and a small mint-green seed-shaped marking at the tail tip. It holds ONE small plain chestnut between its forepaws. Compact nimble body and visible little hind feet. The tail is the defining shape, not generic fluffy fur details. Warm friendly adventurous personality. No hats or clothes.

B (top center) — vine mole, plant element. A low, stocky moss-green burrowing creature with a warm peach little muzzle, kind visible oval dark eyes, cream cheeks, two large shovel-like forepaws, small back feet. A paired tender-leaf forelock grows naturally from the top of its head. ONE short green vine tail curls upward behind it and ends in a small split leaf. It looks earnestly upward, one forepaw slightly raised, ready to help. Broad squat trapezoid body, quiet gentle expression. Keep the eyes and face clearly visible. A plant-and-mammal creature, not a person in a plant suit.

C (top right) — breeze bird, wind element. A perky small teal-and-ivory songbird with coral-orange shoulder feathers, dark turquoise wing edges and a short lemon-yellow beak. A three-feather fan crest points diagonally backward; a broad semicircular tail fan has three conspicuous alternating teal and ivory blades. One foot lifted and the wings slightly spread as if testing a breeze. Slim pear-shaped body and confident curious eyes. Distinctly a bird with two wings and two little legs, no mammal arms.

D (bottom left) — stream otter, water element. A cheerful pale slate-blue and ivory otter-like creature with a visibly long horizontal flexible body, short sturdy legs, small rounded ears, short whiskers, a broad flattened paddle tail in darker slate. The end of the tail has a simple aqua ripple-shaped marking. Leaning forward playfully with one forepaw raised, body and tail forming an easy readable S curve. Bright relaxed face. Not an upright blue bear, no shields or armor. Its streamlined horizontal silhouette must clearly differ from the squirrel and dormouse.

E (bottom center) — coil dormouse, electric element. A shy compact dormouse with pale lavender-gray fur, rich plum-brown back, an ivory muzzle and belly, small rounded ears with copper inner coloring, half-open amber eyes and a gentle closed-mouth smile. Its ONE long smooth tail forms a large open spiral to one side; three copper-colored bands grow as natural markings on that tail, separated by small mint-blue highlights, subtly suggesting stored static electricity. A small static spark appears at the tail tip only. Low tucked sitting posture and tiny forepaws resting together, soft sleepy personality. No cheek circles, no lightning-bolt tail, no black ear tips, no yellow body, no mechanical components or jewelry.

F (bottom right) — dusk glider, dark element. A friendly nocturnal flying-squirrel-like creature gliding in a shallow frontal three-quarter pose. Deep indigo upper fur, dusty violet gliding membranes, ivory cheeks and belly, large warm amber eyes framed by soft dark feather-shaped eye-mask markings. Rounded mammalian ears, a softly pointed muzzle, ONE long flowing tail with an ivory tip. Its gliding membrane connects the sides of its front legs to its hind legs; exactly four limbs, no separate wings. Calm observant expression, charming rather than scary. Fully show the tail and membrane silhouette. No rings or forehead symbol.

Composition quality: all six comparable visual scale and illustration finish, balanced negative space, silhouettes readable independently. Maintain differences in head shape, eye design, expression and posture. A fluffy upright, B squat low, C feathered upright, D long horizontal, E tucked round with open spiral tail, F broad gliding membrane. Crisp illustrated concept art, no pixelation, no 3D rendering, no photographic fur, no watercolor, no merchandise mockup.
